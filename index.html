Other HadUK-Grid variables

Monthly context is available for mean wind speed at 10 m (knots), relative humidity (%), sunshine duration (hours), vapour pressure (hPa) and mean sea level pressure (hPa), as well as frost and snow counts. These variables have not been extracted for this prototype.

Wind and humidity could add context for leaf drying, dispersal and moisture conditions. Monthly summaries must remain separate from preceding 7/28-day weather windows; they cannot resolve individual pre-sampling events. A calendar-month average may also include days after sampling.

Methods, provenance & release boundaries

HadUK-Grid v1.3.2.ceda; corrected-date outputs from research commit 128d3df. Fonthill and Sherborne winter Y1 dates are 14 December 2020. Weather is gridded context, not a field sensor measurement. Sampling time is unknown; windows use the workflow's midnight cutoff and aligned observation periods.

Microbial aggregation: tape fractions → equal pool-side means → equal paired-side biological-pool means → equal pools within cultivar event → equal observed cultivars within campaign → equal campaigns. Repeated quadrats do not become independent site replicates. Prevalence is balanced positive-read detection. Relative sequence-read abundance is affected by amplification and marker copy number; it is not organism abundance.

Top 15 resolved genera must retain all unresolved lineages and Other. Comparisons must show shared campaigns, cultivar population and sample coverage. “Detected only at this site in retained ground-proximal paired samples” does not establish absence elsewhere; default to repeated campaign detection.

Zymoseptoria links open the Z. tritici Wikipedia page for background reading, not species identification. The reviewed dominant ITS ASV is Zymoseptoria tritici / Z. pseudotritici — this ITS marker cannot distinguish between these two species. Other ASVs retain their individual reviewed annotations. Formal taxonomy, reviewed labels and stable ASV identity must remain separate.

DA is presented separately as exploratory study-wide contrasts; soil–leaf comparisons are deferred. No research process was started or interrupted. No publication is approved.
